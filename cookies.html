🍪 Mindful Bites: Our Cookies Philosophy

Just like a honeybee sips nectar without harm, our cookies enhance your journey while respecting your choices. Easy, transparent, and always under your control.

Cookies Policy

Last updated: April 2025 — Gentle Bloom Edition

At YarrowBloom Sanctuary (“we”, “us”, “our”), we believe in honest transparency — whether in our gardens or on our website. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences. By continuing to use our Site, you agree to our use of cookies as described, unless you have set your browser to reject them. But don’t worry — you’re always in the gardener’s seat.

🌾 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit websites. They help us remember your preferences, understand how visitors interact with our content, and ensure the site works smoothly. Think of them as tiny compost helpers — invisible but useful, and never intrusive.

🍃 2. How We Use Cookies at YarrowBloom Sanctuary

We use only minimal, respectful cookies — nothing like the industrial tracking gardens. Our cookies fall into these categories:

🌼 Essential Cookies
Needed for site functionality (e.g., security, navigation, form submissions). No consent required, but always benign.
📊 Preference Cookies
Remember your language or display choices (like dark/light mode — not active yet but future friendly).
📈 Analytics Cookies
We use lightweight analytics (like plausible or anonymized counters) to see which garden articles inspire you most — no personal identifiers.
🌿 Functional
Help with embedded content (e.g., a workshop calendar) — but only if you interact.

Importantly: We never use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or share cookie data with third-party advertisers. Your digital footprints stay within our sanctuary grounds.

📜 3. Specific Cookies We May Place

  • cookie_consent_preference: Stores your cookie consent choice (lifetime: 6 months).
  • session_id: Temporary cookie that expires when you close your browser, used to recognize you during a single site visit.
  • preferred_language: If you were to select a language (English default) — stores choice for 1 year.
  • visit_source: Anonymized, non-personal referrer info to improve garden paths (deleted after 30 days).
🧺 No 'forever' cookies: All cookies set by YarrowBloom Sanctuary have limited lifetimes — because nature renews itself, so does your data footprint.

⚙️ 4. Your Control – Manage Cookie Preferences

You are the guardian of your own digital garden. You can:

  • Opt-out of analytics cookies via our preference tool (see button below).
  • Set your browser to block cookies or alert you when cookies are sent. However, disabling essential cookies may affect some features (like completing a workshop registration).
  • Withdraw consent anytime by clearing your cookies or changing settings below.

Click the button above to update your consent preferences for analytics and non-essential cookies. We’ll remember your choice.

🔗 5. Third-Party Cookies & External Links

Occasionally we embed content from trusted partners (e.g., a virtual garden tour via Vimeo, or plant identification tool). These third-party services may set their own cookies. We do not control those cookies, and we encourage you to review their policies. However, we only embed services that align with our privacy ethos. Examples: Vimeo (privacy-respecting video), and open-source map tiles — no Google Analytics or Facebook pixels.

Our website may contain links to other nature-oriented sites. This Cookies Policy does not apply to them.

🍃 6. Consent & Legal Basis

When you first visit our website, a cookie banner will ask for your consent for non-essential cookies (analytics and preference cookies). By clicking “Accept All” or “Accept Necessary Only” you give (or withhold) your permission. You can change your mind anytime using the button above. Essential cookies are always active because they make the site work properly — they don’t collect personal data for tracking.

🌸 GDPR & ePrivacy aligned: We follow European and US state privacy principles. Your cookie choices are fully respected and stored securely.

🌼 7. Updates to This Cookies Policy

Occasionally, we may update our cookie practices to reflect new features or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. If changes are significant, we’ll notify you via a notice on our homepage. Your continued use means acceptance of the revised policy.

🧑‍🌾 Frequently Asked: Cookie Clarity Corner

❓ Do cookies mean you can see my personal identity?
No. Our cookies never store your name, email, or address. They only store anonymous IDs or functional preferences.

❓ Can I still browse if I disable all cookies?
Absolutely! You will still enjoy our garden gallery, blog, and articles. Only interactive forms might lose some convenience (eg. language memory).

❓ How often do you review your cookie list?
We perform a cookie audit twice a year and whenever we add new digital features. Transparency is part of our sanctuary promise.

❓ Do you use fingerprinting or supercookies?
Never. We find those invasive, just like plastic in a garden.
